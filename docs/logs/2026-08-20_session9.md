## Part 1 — Federal PSOB cancer presumption identified and documented

Peter brought in a PDF of S. 237, the "Honoring Our Fallen Heroes Act of 2025," asking whether
the dashboard needs a federal-coverage layer.

**The bill is law, and the PDF was the wrong document.** It is the RS version — reported out of
Senate Judiciary by Grassley on 2025-05-20, without amendment. The provision was folded into
the FY2026 NDAA and enacted as **P.L. 119-60 § 8205**, signed **2025-12-18**, codified at
**34 U.S.C. § 10281(q)**, 139 Stat. 1846–1850.

Three citation traps the bill version would have caused:

1. **There is no "Honoring Our Fallen Heroes Act."** S. 237 § 1 would have enacted that short
   title; it was dropped on enactment. The phrase survives only as a section heading.
2. **The subsection is (q), not (p).** § 8204 of the same public law — an unrelated PSOB
   expansion for retired LEOs killed in targeted attacks, retroactive to 2012-01-01 — took (p)
   first. A citation to § 10281(p) points at a different provision.
3. **Signed 2025-12-18**, per the govinfo approved date. Several congressional press releases
   say the 19th.

Operative language of the presumption, cancer list, eligibility gates, rebuttal standard,
update mechanism, and retroactivity is **verbatim identical** between S. 237 RS and the enacted
text — verified by direct comparison. Differences are structural (no short title, (p)→(q), the
SAFR "line of duty action" definition folded in as § 8205(d)) plus one addition: **§ 8205(e),
a permanent $255M rescission from the DOJ Assets Forfeiture Fund**, a pay-for not in the bill.

**Substance:** rebuttable presumption that carcinogen exposure is a line-of-duty injury for
PSOB death / permanent-total-disability benefits. 20 enumerated cancers, plus any WTC-related
cancer under PHSA § 3312(a), plus a mandatory 3-year review cycle and a citizen-petition
mechanism with statutory deadlines (180-day referral, 30-day congressional notification).
Gates: 5 years service before diagnosis, diagnosis within 15 years of last active service,
rebuttable only on evidence exposure was *not a substantial contributing factor*. Retroactive
to deaths/claims on or after 2020-01-01, with a **3-year filing window closing ~2028-12-18**.

**The finding that matters for this project:** the statute says **"public safety officer,"**
which under the PSOB definitions includes law enforcement — not firefighters only, despite
uniformly fire-service framing in the press and advocacy coverage. Federal law now treats
cancer as occupational for LEOs while most state cancer presumptions in our data cover
firefighters only. That is a documented federal floor above a patchy state ceiling, and it is
a much stronger version of the LEO coverage-gap argument.

**Written:**
- `docs/admin/federal-psob-cancer-presumption-2025.md` — citation, operative text, gates,
  enacted-vs-bill diff, dashboard implications.
- `docs/lit/federal/pl119-60_sec8204-8205_enacted.txt` — the operative text, extracted from
  the govinfo P.L. 119-60 HTML with page markers and marginal NOTE tags stripped.
- `docs/lit/federal/klobuchar_2025_s237_psob_exposure_cancers.pdf` + `.lit/` extract — the
  superseded bill, kept because it is the version in circulation.

`docs/lit/` is gitignored, so the analytical note deliberately lives in `docs/admin/` where it
survives to the other machine. **The federal layer itself is not built** — decided to be
separate work (see Part 2's reasoning).

## Part 2 — Data pipeline rebuilt on research-derived provenance

### What was actually wrong

`bob.md` had carried this since 2026-07-02 as "the CSV has diverged from the JSON." Measured,
that framing was wrong and the real defect was worse.

- **Schema drift was one column** (`source_url`, from P7). CSV had 20 fields, JSON 21.
- **Nothing had been lost.** Keyed on state × condition × responder: 384 cells in the CSV, 438
  in the JSON, CSV-only set **empty**. The 54 new cells were the P4/P5 completeness fills.
  Of 402 matched rows, 91 had any field change, concentrated in `notes` (76, from P8) and
  `presumption_type` (37). Forward-porting was mechanical.
- **`4_iaff_combine.r` would have corrupted the data if run.** Its
  `valid_ptype <- c("statute","EO","expired","none")` silently mapped anything else to `"none"`.
  The live data contains `pension` (28), `employer_benefit` (26), `lodd_only` (4) — **58 rows
  would have been blanked with no warning.** `app.R` (lines 29–65) carries all seven values,
  making **`app.R` the de facto schema authority and script 4 stale.**

So the defect was **provenance and reproducibility**, not schema drift: the pipeline's source
was the IAFF scrape, which P8 had demoted from ground truth, and nothing could regenerate the
479-row JSON from any input. It was hand-built by eight sequential Python patches.

### The template was stranded, and the fix was not obvious

`docs/lit/state_histories/templates/state_template.md` could not be committed: `docs/lit/` was
ignored wholesale as "Copyrighted PDFs," so the template — which defines the record schema
`legislative-researcher` must emit — existed on this machine only. That is the same failure mode
that desynced `state_histories/` from June to August, and it would have meant the other machine
could not run a state review correctly.

**The ignore pattern had to change from `docs/lit/` to `docs/lit/**`.** Git never descends into a
directory excluded by a plain trailing-slash pattern, which makes any negation inside it
unreachable no matter how it is written. With `/**` the negations apply and the exception works.
That reasoning is in the `.gitignore` comment, because it otherwise reads like a typo.

Verified with `git check-ignore` that the exception is genuinely narrow: the template is
trackable; `registry.md`, all 53 `*_timeline.md` files, and everything in `docs/lit/federal/`
remain ignored. `git add -A --dry-run` staged exactly two paths.

**Publishing the timeline files and `registry.md` was raised and deliberately deferred** — that
would make the pipeline fully reproducible from a clean checkout and close the sync gap for
good, but it publishes the full 50-state research output, which is a decision about the work

**Why these three matter here.** LaSee is Washington State's own presumption-claims
baseline (330 claims 2000–2017: heart 43%, cancer 35%, respiratory 15%, infectious 8%),

excludes LE. Neugent is NCCI's insurer-side ratemaking view with a jurisdiction chart

the state timeline schema. Brandt-Rauf is a 50-state inventory for first-responder
**mental health** specifically: only nine states have a causation presumption, against
the physical-mental / mental-physical / mental-mental taxonomy most statutes turn on.

### The LaSee PDF was corrupt and had to be repaired

Worth recording because nothing about it was obvious. Every extractor returned **zero
words** with `MuPDF error: zlib error: incorrect header check`.

Cause: 18 objects were wrapped in bogus `<</Filter/FlateDecode/Length N>>stream…endstream`
envelopes around payloads that were **never compressed**, so every reader tried to inflate
plaintext. The trailer also carried `/Lambkin 307519` where a valid file wants `/XRefStm`
— the same seven characters, so not random corruption. Producer string shows iTextSharp.

Two fixes that did **not** work, in case this recurs: patching `/Lambkin` back to
`/XRefStm` (the xref stream it points at is itself unreadable), and rebuilding the xref
table from an object scan (offsets were never the problem).

What worked: unwrap the false stream envelopes, emit each payload as a plain object body,
then rebuild the xref and trailer over the rewritten objects. Recovered 37,917 characters
across all 6 pages; verified by rendering page 1 and reading it, not by trusting the
character count. Generalized note added to `rules/environment-gotchas.md`.

**The copy in `docs/lit/` is a repaired reconstruction, not the publisher's file.** It
renders faithfully and no content appears lost, but it is a rebuilt file.

**No unmodified original survives.** The source at `~/Desktop/workers_comp/` was gone by
end of session — not removed by any step recorded here, which only ever read it. So the
reconstruction is currently the only copy of this article in the project.
