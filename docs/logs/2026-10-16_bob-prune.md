- **2026-08-20 — federal cancer presumption identified and documented; dashboard needs a federal layer.** Peter brought in a PDF of S. 237 (the "Honoring Our Fallen Heroes Act of 2025"). It is law: folded into the FY2026 NDAA and signed **2025-12-18** as **P.L. 119-60 § 8205**, codified at **34 U.S.C. § 10281(q)**. Establishes a rebuttable presumption that carcinogen exposure is a line-of-duty injury for PSOB death/permanent-total-disability benefits — 20 enumerated cancers plus WTC-related cancers plus a mandatory 3-year review cycle and a citizen-petition mechanism with statutory deadlines. Covers **"public safety officer,"** which includes law enforcement, not firefighters only, despite universal fire-service framing in the press. Retroactive to deaths/claims on or after 2020-01-01 with a hard filing deadline of ~2028-12-18. Full write-up, citation traps, and enacted-vs-bill diff: `docs/admin/federal-psob-cancer-presumption-2025.md`. Source docs in `docs/lit/federal/` (gitignored — see that note's sync warning). **Design since decided (2026-08-20):** federal coverage goes in its own file (`website/data/federal_coverage.json`) with its own schema and a separate display, *not* as a 51st row. PSOB is a lump-sum death/disability benefit, not workers' comp, so a shared array would imply federal coverage substitutes for state coverage; and `app.R` keys the map and ~12 counts on `state`, where a "US" pseudo-state has no map polygon. Implementation not started, but no longer blocked — the pipeline rebuild above cleared the way.
